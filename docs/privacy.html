Privacy Policy — LifeStyle

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to LifeStyle ("the App", "we", "our"), a product review video creation tool. It describes how LifeStyle collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to LifeStyle, we collect and store:

  • Your TikTok access token (used to upload videos on your behalf)
  • Your TikTok display name (used to show your connection status)

We do not collect your TikTok password, follower data, existing videos, or any other personal information from TikTok.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your TikTok credentials are used solely to upload video content that you create and explicitly choose to publish through LifeStyle. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your TikTok access token is transmitted only to TikTok's API servers for the purpose of uploading your content.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your access token is stored locally on the server running the application. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from the Settings page. Upon disconnection, your stored access token is deleted immediately. No TikTok-related data is retained after disconnection.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at namvu210+lifestyle.app@gmail.com or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
