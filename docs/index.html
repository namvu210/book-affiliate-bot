🎬 LifeStyle

Create engaging product review videos for TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube with AI assistance

Features

📝 AI Review Drafting

LifeStyle uses AI to help you draft product review scripts tailored to your audience. You always review and edit before publishing.

🎥 Video Rendering

Combine product images, voiceover narration, and animated subtitles into short-form videos optimized for social media.

🎵 Voice Narration

Generate natural-sounding voiceovers in Vietnamese using text-to-speech technology. Multiple voice styles available.

📱 Multi-Platform Publishing

Share your review videos directly to TikTok, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts from one place.

How It Works

Add Your Product

Paste a product link or import product information. LifeStyle extracts title, description, price, and customer reviews.

Generate Review Script

LifeStyle drafts a review based on real product information and customer feedback. You can edit, regenerate, or customize the tone.

Create Video

Add product images, select background music, and LifeStyle renders a short video with voiceover and subtitles.

Publish

Share directly to your connected social media accounts, or download the video to post manually.

About LifeStyle

LifeStyle is a content creation tool designed for social media creators and affiliate marketers. It streamlines the process of creating product review videos — from script writing to video rendering to publishing. LifeStyle helps creators produce consistent, engaging content without requiring video editing skills.

Built for the Vietnamese market, LifeStyle supports Vietnamese language content, local e-commerce platforms, and popular social media channels used in Vietnam.
