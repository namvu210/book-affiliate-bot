## Video Generation Performance
Current: ~45-60s per video (2 products × 2 personas × 2 platforms = 8 videos ≈ 8 min)

Bottlenecks (per video):
  1. ffmpeg segment encoding: ~15-20s (one segment per image, sequential)
  2. Music fetch from Freesound: ~3-5s (first video only, cached after)
  3. TTS generation: ~5-10s (ElevenLabs API)
  4. AI image generation: ~15s per image (Gemini image model)
  5. Image pre-filter: ~3s per product photo (new, runs once per product)

Improvement options:
  - [ ] Parallel ffmpeg segments: encode image segments in parallel (subprocess pool)
        → Estimated savings: 10-15s per video
  - [ ] Pre-generate all audio before video step (batch TTS)
        → Audio already exists from Step 2, but re-generated if voice_speed differs
  - [ ] Cache AI images across personas of same product
        → Same product photos → reuse scene descriptions + filtered images
  - [ ] Reduce ffmpeg quality for preview (lower resolution, faster preset)
  - [ ] Use ffmpeg concat demuxer with image duration instead of per-segment encoding
        → Single ffmpeg call instead of N+2 calls
        → Estimated savings: 20-30s per video (biggest win)
  - [ ] Pre-cache music per genre (download 5 tracks per genre on first run)
