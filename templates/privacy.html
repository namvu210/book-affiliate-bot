Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we store your access token to publish videos on your behalf. We also store your TikTok display name to show your connection status.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your TikTok credentials are used solely to upload videos you create in our app. We do not access your existing TikTok content, followers, or personal information beyond what is needed for publishing.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Data Deletion

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from the settings page. This deletes your stored access token immediately.
